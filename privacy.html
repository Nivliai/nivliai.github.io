Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Nivli is an AI assistant for questions, writing, learning, and code. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep it short on purpose.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture. We never see your Google password.
  • Your conversations. The messages you send, the replies you receive, and any images or text files you attach.
  • Personalization. Answers from setup, your saved memory, custom instructions, and your chosen username.
  • Preferences on your device. Theme, text size, and your selected model are stored in your browser.
  • Basic technical data. Standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and error reports, used to keep Nivli running and secure.

How we use it

  • To generate replies and keep your chat history available to you.
  • To apply your memory and instructions when you turn them on.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve Nivli, including fixing errors and preventing abuse.
  • To contact you about your account when needed.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your conversations for advertising.

Who processes your data

To answer you, Nivli sends your messages, attachments, and any memory or instructions you have enabled to the AI model providers and hosting services we use. When a question needs current information, the search terms are sent to a web search provider. These providers process data only to deliver the service to us. We choose them carefully, but their handling of data is also governed by their own terms.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and service.

Memory and personalization

Memory is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, your saved memory is included with your messages. Chat-only memory is used only inside its own conversation. You can edit or clear both at any time.

Retention and deletion

We keep your conversations and account data until you delete them or close your account. You can delete individual chats from the sidebar. To delete your account and the data tied to it, write to us at the address below. Some records may be kept for a limited time for security, legal, or backup reasons.

Security

We use encrypted connections and limit access to the data we store. No online service can promise perfect security, so please avoid sharing passwords, financial details, or other highly sensitive information in your chats.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain uses. Contact us to make a request and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Nivli is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

Cookies and local storage

We use local storage and similar technology to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use advertising cookies.

Changes

If we make a meaningful change to this policy, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
